Guide I

Bag Strap Styles

Five strap styles cover almost every bag. Each changes how a bag sits, how much it can carry and how dressed-up it feels.

IThe chain strap

Metal links, often with a leather ribbon woven through for comfort. Chains suit small evening and day bags, can usually be worn long or doubled short, and look polished with almost anything.

IIThe top handle

A short, often rigid handle above the bag. It is carried in the hand or the crook of the arm and gives a structured, formal look. Many top-handle bags add a detachable long strap for flexibility.

IIIThe crossbody strap

A long, adjustable strap worn diagonally across the body. It keeps hands free, spreads weight across the torso and keeps the bag in front of you in crowds. Look for an adjustable buckle or slider.

IVThe shoulder strap

A medium-length strap that lets the bag sit under the arm. The drop, the distance from the top of the strap to the bag, decides the fit:

  • About 20 to 25 cm: snug under the arm.
  • About 30 to 40 cm: sits at the hip.
  • Wider straps feel lighter on the shoulder.

VThe convertible and the swap

Bags with D-rings and clip hooks can change straps entirely: a chain for evening, a wide woven strap for weekends. It is the easiest way to make one good bag do the work of several.
